# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Patchlens aims to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect differing opinions and experience levels.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issue reports, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainers through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting route in the repository's Security tab instead of a public issue. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
